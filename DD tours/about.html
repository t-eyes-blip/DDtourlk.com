Our Story

DD Tours began in 2020, not as a large company, but as a simple idea shaped by passion, resilience, and a deep love for showcasing the beauty of Sri Lanka.It started with a single purpose to help travellers feel the island, not just see it.

From quiet coastal roads to misty hill country journeys, every route was driven with the belief that travel should feel personal, human, and unforgettable.What started small gradually grew through trust, word of mouth, and meaningful connections with people from around the world.

Today, DD Tours stands as more than a transport service, but it is a journey companion built on care, reliability, and local insight.And while the scale has changed since 2020, the heart behind it remains the same: every traveller deserves a story worth remembering.
